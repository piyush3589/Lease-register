RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on 1st March 2025 between Mr. Arvind Shah (Landlord) and
Ms. Priya Nair (Tenant).

Property Address: Flat No. 1204, Oberoi Splendor, JVLR, Andheri East, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400060

Lease Term: This agreement is valid for a period of 11 months, commencing from 1st April 2025
and ending on 28th February 2026.

Monthly Rent: The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. 65,000 (Rupees Sixty-Five Thousand
only), payable on or before the 5th of each month.

Security Deposit: The Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of Rs. 3,25,000 (Rupees
Three Lakh Twenty-Five Thousand only) at the time of signing this agreement.

Renewal: This lease may be renewed for a further period upon mutual written consent of both
parties, at least 30 days before the expiry of the current term, subject to a rent revision of
not more than 10%.

Maintenance: Society maintenance charges of Rs. 4,500 per month shall be borne by the Tenant
in addition to the rent.
